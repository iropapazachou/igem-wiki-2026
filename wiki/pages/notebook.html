    Overview

    Science rarely follows a perfectly straight path. Behind every successful experiment are rounds of planning, optimisation, troubleshooting, repetition, and sometimes unexpected results that reshape the next step. Throughout the development of AETh3R, we considered careful documentation an essential part of our experimental work.

    Our laboratory notebook records the progress of the iGEM Thessaloniki 2026 Wet Lab team as we investigated our therapeutic approach for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), centred on targeted protein degradation of MDM2 and KRAS. From establishing and optimising our work with A549 cells to evaluating cell viability, refining experimental conditions and studying the molecular effects of our treatments, each stage contributed to the development of our project.

    Beyond recording protocols and results, the notebook also reflects the decisions made along the way: conditions that required optimisation, experiments that had to be repeated, challenges encountered during cell culture and analysis, and the modifications introduced in response to what we observed.

    By documenting both our progress and our setbacks, we aim to provide a transparent and reproducible account of our experimental journey. The following entries present the evolution of our Wet Lab work throughout the season and show how each experiment helped guide the next.
