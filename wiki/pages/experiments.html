    Overview

    Reproducibility is at the heart of reliable scientific research. Throughout the development of AETh3R, our Wet Lab work relied on carefully defined and consistently applied experimental procedures, allowing us to compare results across different conditions and progressively refine our approach.

    This page brings together the main protocols used by the iGEM Thessaloniki 2026 Wet Lab team during our investigation of a targeted protein degradation strategy for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). These protocols supported the different stages of our experimental workflow, including A549 cell culture and maintenance, cell seeding and treatment, viability assessment, protein analysis, and other molecular and biochemical procedures required for the evaluation of our system.

    Whenever necessary, established methods were adapted and optimised according to the needs of our experiments, the characteristics of the A549 cell line, and the equipment and reagents available in our laboratory. These modifications were documented to ensure consistency between experiments and to make our methodology as transparent as possible.

    By compiling our procedures in one place, we aim to provide a clear and reproducible experimental framework that not only documents how our results were generated, but can also serve as a useful reference for future iGEM teams and researchers working on similar experimental systems.
