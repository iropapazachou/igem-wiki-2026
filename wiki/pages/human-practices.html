Overview

Throughout the development of our project "siREN", our team embraced a profound sense of responsibility, particularly given the therapeutic potential of our work for human applications. Recognizing the ethical weight and societal implications of designing a treatment for human use, we prioritized a deep understanding of the problem we sought to address, carefully examining both the bioethical considerations and the broader social challenges tied to our solution. From the earliest stages, we ensured that every aspect of our project development was informed by open dialogue with the community, continuous reflection, and the pursuit of responsible innovation. Human practices were not treated as a separate aspect but were intrinsically linked to every decision we made.

- Pillars of our Methodology -

1. Identifying & Contextualizing the Problem

  • Clinical Need & Patient Burden Mapping
  • Regulatory Bottleneck Assessment

2. Developing & Evolving AETHER

  • Feedback-Driven Wet & Dry Lab Project Development & Refinement

3. Setting Responsible Standards

  • Comprehensive Biosafety Framework & Containment Protocols
  • Ethical Safeguards for Responsible Synthetic Biology Innovation
  • Sustainable Development

4. Designing for Real-World Implementation

  • Entrepreneurial Capacity Building
  • Strategic Business & Scalability Planning
  • Transparent Assessment & Clinical Translation Roadmap

5. Inclusive Engagement & Educational Outreach

  • Psychosocially Grounded Educational Module Design
  • Inclusive and Informed Pedagogical Strategy

6. Driving Systemic & Societal Impact

  • Synergistic Integration of Education, Sustainability, Inclusivity & Entrepreneurship

iGEM Human Practices Maturity Model

We structured our Human Practices strategy around the iGEM Maturity Model to ensure the effective integration of multiple social, ethical and real-world considerations into our project. In this way, we established a direct feedback loop between stakeholder insights and design decisions, as well as moved beyond identifying a problem to anticipating its broader implications, incorporating diverse perspectives and ultimately designing a solution with real-world impact.

Level 1
Reflecting on design decisions
HIGH

Iterative, targeted dialogues with stakeholders, documented in a detailed and transparent way, led to a strategic pivot that dynamically reshaped our project design.

Level 2
Exploring & reflecting on context beyond the lab
HIGH

Rigorous primary research and contextual analysis directly guided our project development while revealing critical policy gaps.

Level 3
Incorporating diverse perspectives
MID-HIGH / HIGH

Structured feedback channels allowed us to synthesize diverse perspectives and directly translate critical expert opinions into actionable design updates.

Level 4
Anticipating positive and negative impacts
HIGH

Proactively established sufficient technical countermeasures to mitigate potential harm.

Level 5
Responding to human practices work — Co-evolution
HIGH

The team's scientific, technical, safety and outreach trajectory continuously evolved alongside our Human Practices insights.

Level 6
Approaching limitations with integrity
HIGH

Transparently documenting and evaluating project limitations allowed us to adapt and create a structured, actionable roadmap for future optimization.
